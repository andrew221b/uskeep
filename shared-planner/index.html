Less back-and-forth.
More getting on with it.

Dates, tasks and longer plans need different kinds of space. Uskeep keeps them close without turning everything into the same list.

What’s happening

Put a date in the calendar. Keep the task that gets you ready for it nearby. The next thing to do stays easy to spot.

What’s still ahead

Give a shared goal a name and record contributions or expenses as you go. Uskeep tracks your entries; it never holds or moves money.

What each of you needs

Shared records belong to the space. Home is personal: each person can choose the shortcuts they want to see first.

Tasks and dates are available to start with. Savings goals are part of Uskeep Plus.

Photos, places and the stories that go with them have a place here too.
