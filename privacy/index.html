Privacy Policy

The short version: your shared life is yours. Uskeep stores the details needed to provide the features you use; it does not sell them or use them for advertising.

1. Who is responsible

Uskeep is operated by __PUBLISHER_NAME__. Postal contact: __PUBLISHER_POSTAL_ADDRESS__. For privacy requests, email __PRIVACY_EMAIL__. For general support, email __SUPPORT_EMAIL__.

2. What we process

Depending on what you use, Uskeep processes account and profile details from sign-in; membership and permissions in your shared space; plans, tasks, lists, calendar dates, goals, expenses, wishes, notes, passes, polls, memories, photos, vault items and time capsules. If you use Baby or Medicine, the app can also process a child’s name and birth date, care events, medicine names, intake quantities, schedules, notes and attachments. You decide whether to enter these optional, potentially sensitive details.

We also process the minimum technical data needed for sync, security and delivery: device push tokens if you enable notifications, offline mutation identifiers, diagnostic errors without the content of your notes, and subscription status. Payment-card details are handled by the app store, not Uskeep. Uskeep does not create accounts for children; adults enter care information on their behalf.

3. Why we process it

We use information to provide the app, sync your records, apply your sharing choices, send reminders you enable, restore purchases, secure accounts, and respond to support or legal requests. Where required by law, we rely on performance of our agreement, legitimate security interests, your consent for optional permissions and sensitive information, or a legal obligation. Turning off optional notifications does not disable your records.

4. Sharing and service providers

Only people with access under the app’s permissions can see a shared record. A relationship, name or profile does not automatically grant access to a baby or medicine record. We use Supabase for authentication, database, functions and private storage when connected services are enabled; RevenueCat for subscription entitlement status; Apple and Google for store purchases and sign-in where chosen; and APNs/FCM for enabled push delivery. Opening an optional map can send the selected map coordinates and your network address to OpenStreetMap. Each provider has its own privacy information.

We do not sell personal information, run advertising trackers, or send children’s care records, doses or notes to a generative-AI service in this build.

5. Security and storage

Connected data travels over TLS and is controlled with database row-level security and private file-storage rules. Shared photos and attachments are not intended as public files. Vault files and time-capsule text are encrypted before upload using a key managed by the service. This is not end-to-end encryption: authorized service infrastructure can access the key to operate the feature. Keep independent copies of irreplaceable or highly sensitive records.

Authenticated sessions, offline changes, device caches and operating-system backups may remain on your device. No online service can promise perfect security or uninterrupted availability.

6. How long information stays

Records generally remain while an account or its shared space is active, subject to backup, security and legal needs. Deleting an account removes that person’s access; shared records may remain for an active partner where the product allows it. When a whole space is removed, associated files are queued for deletion. Backup removal follows the provider’s rotation schedule, which the publisher must verify before launch.

7. Your choices and rights

You can change sharing and notification choices in the app, export available account data, edit records, or request account deletion in Settings or at our account deletion page. Depending on where you live, you may request access, correction, erasure, portability, restriction or objection, and complain to your local data-protection authority. Email __PRIVACY_EMAIL__ with a privacy request. We may verify your identity before acting.

8. Children and changes

Uskeep accounts are for adults. A parent or authorized caregiver may choose to record a child’s care data. We do not knowingly allow children to create their own accounts. We may update this policy as the service evolves and will notify users of material changes where required.
