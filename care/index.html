The useful details.
When you need them.

Care records help you remember what happened. They do not decide what should happen next.

Baby

Log feeds, bottles, sleep and diaper changes. Each entry stays in the timeline you can review later.

Medicine

Record an intake you actually gave, with its time and details. A scheduled reminder does not mark a dose as taken.

Who can see it

Care records have their own sharing controls. Adding someone to your space does not automatically give them access to every care record.

Baby and Medicine are in Uskeep Plus. These are record keeping tools, not medical advice. Verify doses and decisions with a qualified professional.
