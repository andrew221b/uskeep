Account & privacy

Delete your Uskeep account.

You can delete your account in the app under Settings → Account. If you can’t access the app, request deletion here. We verify account ownership before anything is removed.

1. Verify your account

Enter the email address linked to your Uskeep account. If an account can be verified, we’ll send a one-time secure sign-in link. This page does not reveal whether an account exists.

2. Confirm permanent deletion

Deleting your Uskeep account does not cancel App Store or Google Play subscriptions. Cancel separately in your store account to stop future billing. Shared-space content may remain accessible to your authorized partner as described in the Privacy Policy.

Account deletion confirmed

Your Uskeep account and sign-in access have been removed. Secure cleanup of associated private files may continue in the background as described in the Privacy Policy. Store billing, if any, remains managed by Apple or Google Play.

Read our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use. Need help? Contact Uskeep support.
